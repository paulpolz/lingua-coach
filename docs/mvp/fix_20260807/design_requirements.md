## Requirements

1. **Next.js application**
   - Work within the existing Next.js app and preserve its current functionality.
   - Improve the existing basic UI rather than introducing a completely unrelated design system.

2. **Core experience: language-coach chat**
   - The chat is the primary product experience and should receive the most design attention.
   - Design it as a **personal language tutor**, not a generic ChatGPT clone.
   - Make conversations feel approachable, focused, and educational.

3. **Visual direction**
   - Friendly, modern, clean, and trustworthy.
   - Strong visual hierarchy and excellent readability.
   - Avoid generic “AI-generated SaaS” aesthetics.
   - Establish a consistent design language across the application.

4. **Chat UX**
   - Clear distinction between learner and tutor messages.
   - Excellent typography and spacing for long conversations.
   - Make corrections, explanations, translations, examples, and learning suggestions easy to scan.
   - Design thoughtful loading, streaming, empty, error, and conversation states.
   - Make the composer/input area feel central and effortless to use.

5. **Personalization**
   - The interface should communicate that the tutor adapts to the individual learner.
   - Surface relevant context such as language, level, learning goals, or current exercise without cluttering the conversation.

6. **Interactions & motion**
   - Use subtle micro-interactions and animations where they improve comprehension or feedback.
   - Avoid decorative animation that distracts from learning.

7. **Supporting pages**
   - Extend the same design language to profile, usage statistics, settings, and other sections.
   - These pages should feel like part of the same product, while remaining secondary to the chat.

8. **Design system**
   - Establish reusable patterns for typography, spacing, colors, buttons, cards, navigation, forms, chat elements, states, etc.
   - Prefer reusable components over page-specific styling.

9. **Responsive design**
   - The experience should work naturally across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
   - Pay particular attention to the chat composer, navigation, and conversation readability on small screens.

10. **Design quality process**
   - Use **[Taste](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill)** for overall visual direction and design language.
   - Use **[Emil Design Engineering](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills)** for interaction and motion polish.
   - Use **[Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable)** to review and refine the resulting UI.
   - Prioritize usability and learning experience over visual effects.

### Core design principle

> **Make it feel like a great personal tutor, not a chatbot with a pretty interface.**

The design should feel **friendly → clear → personal → focused → polished**.
